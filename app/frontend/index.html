SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How SafeHer-AI Works

The system combines historical crime information, machine learning, GIS and geofencing to generate a location-based risk estimate.

01
📊

Crime Data

Historical district-level crime records are collected and preprocessed.

→
02
🤖

AI Models

K-Means clustering and a temporal MLP model analyse crime patterns.

→
03
🗺️

GIS Risk Layer

Predicted risk information is associated with geographic district boundaries.

→
04
📍

Geofencing

Your coordinates are checked against the mapped risk zones.
